How it works

Lead Decision Intelligence combines a machine-learning model, an explainability layer and a configurable contact strategy into one workflow for relationship managers.

From data to decision

1Lead data

Customer profile, campaign context and interaction history for every lead.

2Validation

Every record is checked for valid values and a consistent contact history.

3Prediction

Gradient boosting estimates the likelihood that the lead subscribes.

4Explanation

TreeSHAP shows exactly which signals raised or lowered that likelihood.

5Strategy

Cost-aware rules turn the likelihood into a next action, with a confidence check.

6Action

The relationship manager sees the action, the reasons and can test what-if scenarios.

Data

UCI Bank Marketing dataset: {{ "{:,}".format(card.data.rows) }} direct-marketing phone contacts of a Portuguese bank, with the outcome of a term-deposit offer. {{ card.features | length }} input fields in three groups:

  • Customer profile: age, job, marital status, education, balance, loans, credit default
  • Campaign context: contact channel, month, day
  • Interaction history: contacts this campaign, contacts before, days since last contact, previous outcome

Model

  • {{ card.model_display_name }}, selected from six approaches (logistic regression, decision tree, random forest, gradient boosting, neural network, 1D CNN)
  • Trained on {{ "{:,}".format(card.data.dev_rows) }} leads with 5-fold cross-validation; evaluated on {{ "{:,}".format(card.data.test_rows) }} unseen leads
  • ROC-AUC {{ "%.3f" | format(card.test_metrics.roc_auc) }}, calibrated probabilities
  • Likely range from {{ card.bootstrap_members }} re-trained model variants

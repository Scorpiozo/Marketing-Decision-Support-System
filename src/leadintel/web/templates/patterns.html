Interaction patterns

In this dataset an interaction pattern is what we can observe about the bank's past contact with a lead: the previous campaign's outcome (poutcome), how many times they were contacted before (previous), how long ago (pdays), and how many times this campaign has already called (campaign). There is no call content, sentiment or transcript, so “interaction” here means contact history, not conversation.

  • Supported: recency × previous outcome is the strongest genuine interaction-history pair. For leads last contacted within ~200 days, recency lifts the estimate 2–3× more after a past success than after a past failure. (Leads last contacted 400+ days ago get a large lift whatever the outcome, but such leads only exist late in the file, so that is partly the drift artifact again.)
  • Not supported: contacts-this-campaign × previous outcome is weak ({{ "%.3f" | format(hyp_campaign) }}). Repeated contact acts mostly on its own.
  • Caution: day × month and channel × month rank high because together they reconstruct when a lead was contacted. That is a data-collection-period effect, not customer behaviour.

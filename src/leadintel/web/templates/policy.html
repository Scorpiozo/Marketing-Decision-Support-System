Business assumptions

How actions are decided

  1. Human review when the model's likely range crosses the follow-up line.
  2. Prioritise when the likelihood is at or above the priority threshold.
  3. Change approach when it is worth a call but the lead is already heavily contacted.
  4. Follow up when likelihood × value exceeds the cost of a call.
  5. Stop repeated follow-up when a call is not worth it and the lead is heavily contacted.
  6. Deprioritise otherwise.
